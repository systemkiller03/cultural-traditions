About the university project

Student ID: 000123
Email: student000123@wiut.uz

I created this project to explore and share some really interesting cultural traditions from around the world. I wanted to make something that is not only informative, but also fun to look through and easy to understand. It was exciting to learn how different cultures celebrate, communicate, and express their identity.

The main idea was to present these traditions in a simple and clear way, while still keeping them engaging. I tried to make each page feel a bit different using colors and images, so it matches the culture being shown.

  • I focused on traditions related to food, rituals, etiquette, music, and public celebrations.
  • Each country page has its own style, but the structure stays consistent so it is easy to navigate.
  • I used images, videos, and sources to make the content more interesting and easier to understand.

Project Info

Focus of the submission

Purpose

The purpose of this project is to show how cultural traditions stay meaningful over time, especially when they are connected to people, places, and shared experiences.

Structure

The website includes five connected pages, making it easy to move between different countries and explore their traditions step by step.

Content

Each page includes a mix of text, images, tables, and videos to show the traditions from different angles and keep the experience interesting.
